# Personalized interview preparation

Create this deliverable after the target JD, the explicit requirement ledger and the candidate evidence ledger are known. Read [role-priority.md](role-priority.md) first. Company research may add cited context, but it is not required. This is preparation, not a prediction of the employer's actual question bank.

## Question selection

Use the smallest useful set of questions that covers the role's `EXPLICIT_CORE` and `STRONG_TEXT_INFERENCE` themes. A `WEAK_TEXT_INFERENCE` may support a supplementary question or a question for the interviewer, but it cannot by itself justify a main practice question.

Every practice question must bind:

```text
at least one JD requirement R
plus candidate evidence E or an explicit evidence gap G
plus optional cited company context C
```

Select the question form from the evidence state:

- direct evidence exists: ask a past-behavior or evidence-deep-dive question;
- only adjacent evidence exists: ask a transferable-experience question and preserve the difference;
- no evidence exists: ask a scenario or gap-verification question instead of assuming the candidate has done the work;
- a requirement is abstract, such as leadership or ownership: translate it into an observable work situation tied to the JD.

Cover the smallest useful set across:

- 60–90 second self-introduction;
- core role responsibilities and technical or functional judgment;
- two or three evidence-rich projects or experiences;
- behavioral questions tied to collaboration, ambiguity, conflict or failure;
- company motivation and product/industry understanding;
- visible resume risks, transitions or weakly evidenced requirements;
- questions the candidate should ask the interviewer.

Do not generate filler questions merely to reach a count. Do not call a question `high probability`, `real company question` or `validated`.

## Question quality gate

A question enters the final outline only when all applicable checks pass:

1. It cites at least one requirement ID and its JD quotation or locator.
2. It names one primary capability or judgment to examine.
3. A past-experience premise cites candidate evidence IDs; otherwise the question is rewritten as transferable or hypothetical.
4. Any company premise has a nearby cited source and is labelled as public fact, company statement or inference.
5. It introduces no unsupported responsibility, metric, result, motivation or personal history.
6. It is meaningfully distinct from the other selected questions.

If a check fails, rewrite the question or remove it. More questions do not compensate for weak grounding.

## Answer format

Use [interview-outline-output.md](../templates/interview-outline-output.md). For every candidate answer include:

1. the JD requirement and whether its priority is explicit or inferred;
2. what the practice question mainly examines;
3. three to five speaking bullets rather than a polished script;
4. evidence IDs or source locations supporting those bullets;
5. likely follow-up questions;
6. `需要本人补充` for motivations, opinions or missing facts that only the user can supply.

STAR may organize experience answers, but omit empty labels and do not force every question into STAR. Preserve the real category of projects, courses, internships and employment.

## Grounding rules

- Resume facts come only from the evidence ledger or explicit user confirmation.
- Company claims require nearby citations from the research record; company context is optional, never a prerequisite for a role-relevant question.
- Never manufacture a failure, conflict, leadership role, result, motivation, salary position or employer priority.
- New facts stated during practice remain `需要本人确认`; do not write them into the resume or use them as established premises.
- Never send candidate identity, contact details, resume wording or private employer information to a search engine.
- Do not write word-for-word scripts that encourage robotic memorization.
- Flag sensitive topics such as compensation, termination, health, family status or protected characteristics instead of speculating.
