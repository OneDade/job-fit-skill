# Personalized interview preparation

Create this deliverable only after the target JD, evidence ledger and available company research are known. It is preparation, not a prediction of the employer's actual question bank.

## Question selection

Cover the smallest useful set across:

- 60–90 second self-introduction;
- core role responsibilities and technical or functional judgment;
- two or three evidence-rich projects or experiences;
- behavioral questions tied to collaboration, ambiguity, conflict or failure;
- company motivation and product/industry understanding;
- visible resume risks, transitions or weakly evidenced requirements;
- questions the candidate should ask the interviewer.

Prioritize questions that connect at least two of: a JD requirement, candidate evidence, and a cited company fact. Do not generate filler questions merely to reach a count.

## Answer format

Use [interview-outline-output.md](../templates/interview-outline-output.md). For every candidate answer include:

1. what the interviewer is testing;
2. three to five speaking bullets rather than a polished script;
3. evidence IDs or source locations supporting those bullets;
4. likely follow-up questions;
5. `需要本人补充` for motivations, opinions or missing facts that only the user can supply.

STAR may organize experience answers, but omit empty labels and do not force every question into STAR. Preserve the real category of projects, courses, internships and employment.

## Grounding rules

- Resume facts come only from the evidence ledger or explicit user confirmation.
- Company claims require nearby citations from the research record.
- Never manufacture a failure, conflict, leadership role, result, motivation or salary position.
- Do not write word-for-word scripts that encourage robotic memorization.
- Flag sensitive topics such as compensation, termination, health, family status or protected characteristics instead of speculating.
