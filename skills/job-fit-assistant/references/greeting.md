# First-message greetings (打招呼语)

Use this stage when the user asks for 打招呼语、开场白、给 HR 发的第一条消息、Boss 直聘招呼, or a short LinkedIn/InMail note for one supplied JD. It is a short chat message, not a cover letter.

## Inputs

Build or reuse the requirement ledger and evidence ledger first (see [portable-workflow.md](portable-workflow.md)). A greeting may use only:

- the JD's public title and explicit requirements;
- candidate facts in the evidence ledger, or facts the user confirmed in this conversation or in a confirmed-facts file;
- cited company facts, only when company research was already done.

## Selection

Pick the two or three strongest `MATCHED` requirements, preferring `EXPLICIT_CORE` ones. Use `TRANSFERABLE` evidence only with honest wording such as `做过相关的…` or `在课程项目中…`. Never mention a `MISSING` or `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` requirement as if it were met.

## Output

Produce three versions the user can paste directly. Each must:

- stay within 100 Chinese characters (or about 60 English words) including punctuation, because job apps truncate the preview;
- open with the target role, then one or two concrete evidence points, then a light call to action (for example `方便的话想发一份简历给您`);
- keep the real category of each fact (course, internship, personal project, employment);
- contain no number, title, employer or result that is absent from the evidence;
- contain no phone number, email or other contact details unless the user asks.

Vary the three versions by angle, not by adjectives:

1. **岗位匹配型** — lead with the most central matched requirement.
2. **项目证据型** — lead with one concrete project or result from the evidence.
3. **简短礼貌型** — 40–60 characters for high-volume sending.

After the three versions, list the evidence IDs each one relies on in one short line, and flag any phrase that needs the user's confirmation. Do not add generic flattery about the company, and do not claim enthusiasm, availability, salary expectations or relocation willingness the user has not stated; put those in `需要你补充` instead.

Use [greeting-output.md](../templates/greeting-output.md) for the layout.
